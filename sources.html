Source Discovery Tips

  • Google Dorks: Use site:linkedin.com/in "Manufacturing" "Malaysia" combined with job title operators ("Director" | "Manager" | "Head of") to surface decision-makers who may not appear in Sales Navigator.
  • FMM Directory: The Federation of Malaysian Manufacturers lists key company contacts, including managing directors and procurement heads — cross-reference names with LinkedIn to find email patterns.
  • Bursa Malaysia Annual Reports: Publicly listed companies disclose board members and senior leadership in their annual reports — extract names, then use Hunter.io to find corporate email formats.
  • SSM e-Info: Company registration search reveals directors and secretaries — ideal for verifying decision-maker names before outreach. Note: requires manual lookup per company.
  • Multi-channel verification: Always verify a prospect's email through at least two sources (e.g. Hunter.io + manual format guess + phone call) before including them in a campaign — bounce rates hurt sender reputation.
